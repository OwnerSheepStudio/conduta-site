// CHAPTER 01 // THE ARCHIVE RECORDS

The Collapse

RECORD Nº 001 // ORIGIN OF THE SHELTER // FULL TRANSCRIPT

The world did not end in a flash.
It ended in silence.

First came the conflicting reports. Then the blackouts. When Russia and the United States pressed their respective buttons, the northern sky burned white. Brazil was never hit by a missile. The country came apart all the same. With no international trade, the ports turned into graveyards of ships. Hunger is faster than radiation.

The shelters appeared as a temporary measure: sealed subway stations, repurposed tunnels, gated blocks converted into fortresses. Out in the abandoned streets, four kinds of people came to define the new Brazil.

Because in the end the nuclear war was only the trigger. The real collapse started inside people.

And that is where this story begins. Not with heroes. Not with villains. With decisions. With conduct.

// PROTOCOL // MATCH CYCLE

How to Play

Five steps. A cycle that repeats until someone wins or dies trying.

1

Pick your character

Your public identity before the shelter.

2

Secret draw

Team, conduct and ability are dealt under seal.

3

Night

Secret actions: investigate, protect, sabotage, abduct.

4

Day

Debate, social reads and a vote. Someone goes to the gallows.

5

Repeat

Night and day cycles until one team meets its objective.

Conduct is not the same as team. It is a social clue, never absolute proof.

// MORAL LAYER // SOCIAL READING

The Three Conducts

A reading layer above the vote. Colour never stands alone: always colour, icon and text.

▲ Order

Signals a tie to the survivor camp and tends to inspire trust. It can still be exploited by manipulation abilities.

▲ Chaos

Points to the oppressor camp. It reads as a high-risk signal once revealed or inferred.

● Unknown

It can belong to any alignment, Fanatic and Mercenary included. The most ambiguous conduct at the table.

// ARSENAL // DEALT BY THE DRAW

Abilities

18 secret abilities, dealt by the draw. These are the ones the archive has released:

Order

Deep Investigation

Mark a target at night and receive their real team, in private, at dawn.

Execution

Mid-day, with no vote and no warning: an immediate elimination in broad daylight.

Revive

During the night, bring an eliminated player back to the table.

Unknown Conduct

Forgery

Mark a dead player and use a temporary copy of their ability next round.

Secret Ballot

Once per match, turn the public vote into an anonymous ballot. Nobody sees who voted for whom.

Last Words

File a message that will be published automatically the moment you die.

Chaos

Abduction

Take someone out of circulation: the target vanishes from the day and is eliminated at nightfall.

Data Tampering

Inject a false conduct into the target's file. From then on, every analysis reads the lie.

Infection

A social time bomb: the infected dies on their own three nights later, with no vote.

FILE Nº 04 // DOSSIER RELEASED

FILE Nº 05 // DOSSIER RELEASED
